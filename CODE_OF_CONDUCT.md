# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to make a small tool work well. Take part in good faith.

- Be direct and kind. Criticize the code, not the person.
- Assume the other person is trying to help, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information. This includes session paths and transcript content from other people's machines.
- Keep discussion on the issue or pull request it belongs to.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a conduct problem, use a [private security advisory](https://github.com/tomimandalaputra/session-ledger/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct, or contact the maintainer through the profile at https://github.com/tomimandalaputra.
